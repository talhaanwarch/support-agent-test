STORE POLICY — Kestrel Outdoor (online outdoor-gear store)

Returns
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, with original tags attached. Worn or used items cannot be returned unless they are defective.
- Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Return shipping is free for defective items. For any other return, a $6.95 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

Refunds
- Refunds go to the original payment method only. No store credit unless the customer asks for it.
- Refunds are issued 5–7 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.

Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and is free on orders over $75; otherwise $5.95.
- Express shipping takes 1–2 business days and costs $15.
- We ship to the United States and Canada only.

Orders
- An order can be cancelled or changed within 1 hour of placing it, from the order confirmation email. After that it is already being packed.
- Support agents cannot see order status. Customers track orders with the tracking link in their shipping email.

Warranty
- Tents and backpacks have a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

Prices
- We do not price-match other stores.
- We do not issue discount codes through support.

Human support
- Human team: support@kestrel-outdoor.example, replies within 1 business day.
